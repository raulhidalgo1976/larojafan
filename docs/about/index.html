About LaRojaFan

LaRojaFan is an independent, unofficial archive of Spain national football team matches and records.

Data sources and limitations

Match results and scorer events are built from the community-maintained international_results dataset. Historical coverage, event details and future fixtures may be incomplete or inaccurate. Goals in our dataset must not be interpreted as verified career totals.

Corrections

For corrections, please contact the site operator through the project repository. Official records should be verified with the RFEF, FIFA or UEFA.
