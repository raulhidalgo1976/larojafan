Match history

Spain defeated Portugal 3–0 in Seville on 16 December 1923. José Luis Zabala scored all three goals, recording the first hat-trick in Spain's senior international history.

Historical notes

RFEF articles disagree on the minute of Zabala's third goal (60th or 70th). The hat-trick itself is documented, but no disputed goal minute is asserted here.
